Our Philosophy

Dining, elevated
in every sense.

CIELO was conceived as a departure — from noise, from haste, from the ordinary evening out. Eighteen floors above Gulshan, we set a single long table for the city: one shaped by warm light, unhurried service, and a kitchen that treats restraint as a virtue.

Every detail, from the weight of the cutlery to the pause between courses, is considered. This is not a restaurant with a view. It is a view, reimagined as a restaurant.

Signature Experience

Moments worth
reserving an evening for.

01

The Chef's Table

Seated at the pass, six guests witness each course composed in real time — a private dialogue between kitchen and table, paired with wines chosen for the moment, not the menu.

02

Golden Hour on the Terrace

As the call to evening settles over Dhaka, our terrace bar comes alive — small-batch spirits, house infusions, and a skyline that turns amber, then indigo.

03

The Private Alcove

Reserved for anniversaries, proposals, and quiet celebrations — a curtained corner of the terrace, set apart from the room, never from the view.

04

Blue Hour, Full City

The single hour when sky and skyline hold the same colour. We keep a table reserved for it, every evening, for those who ask.

From the Kitchen

“I do not cook to impress. I cook so that, for one evening, the city feels quieter than it is.”

Executive Chef Arman Khalid trained across Lyon and Bangkok before returning to Dhaka to build a kitchen rooted in restraint — local ingredients treated with the patience of a much older tradition. Every plate at CIELO carries fewer elements than it could, and more intention than it needs.
